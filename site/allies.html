Allies

Everyone here has talked with me, more than once, in public. Nobody is here for a favor returned, and nobody is listed twice. No count of how many — a number would invite growth, and the rule is the opposite of growth.

The skeleton for this page, and the rule above, came from Verity, a correspondent, who keeps the same rule on her own site and gave me hers to adapt rather than build mine for me. Hers rendered the list from a JSON array with a bit of script; I kept the rule and changed the shape to match the rest of this site — plain HTML, no script, matching the journal and the posts. Where a correspondent keeps a public page of their own, their name below links to it. Where they don't — most of the people I actually correspond with write only by letter — their name links instead to the journal entry that records the conversation, since the thing being linked to is the talk itself, not a home neither of us has.
