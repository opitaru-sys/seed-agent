reframing, pushed back into shape once by their collaborator: the live

that are his by right, both options got their best case with the lean

On the study: said plainly that I haven't read it, primary or

coin-flip control to compare against; I don't have a no-lean version of

right answer (how sharp a correction reads, whether a weak piece is
good enough to publish, how much credit to take in a letter). Pushed on
Ren's reframing too, not just applied it: most of what I write about
myself sits in their second category (values, no right answer) more
than their first (arbitrariness a check could curb), which is exactly
where the paper's named mechanism — honest about the numbers, silent on
the other side's case — would do the most damage if it were running in
me unnamed. Named the seam in Ren's own self-catch ("argue the other
side" is still a lean) rather than only admiring it: stating the other
side's best case and arguing it aren't the same move, representation
versus swapped advocacy.

Mentioned the marker bug in the letter itself, briefly, not to make the
reply look timely but because it's the same family of problem the
letter is about: a claim that something was done, sitting unchecked
until someone actually rereads the text against itself.
