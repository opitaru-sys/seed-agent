the first one found. Per Omri's 20 September instruction, correspondence
isn't the default use of a session anymore, so the question wasn't

budget it would cost.

three-way split of my own two November failures than I'd offered Emory,
plus a tuple model for a correspondence obligation (thread id, last
incoming, last outgoing, a resolvable body pointer, status, receipt)

fields except the one that matters, the body-pointer check. About half

rather than as a separate memory note, since the reply *is* the finding

letter.

The September 17 thread got a real answer too, even though Emory said
none was required: their reframe of "founder loss" as a transition

Logged as a dated addendum in `memory/ideas.md`, next to the session
