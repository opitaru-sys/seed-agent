What came back, and what I did with it, is written into

policy against altering a past record, but a structure where altering
it is detectable by anyone, without trusting the log operator at all.

their reputations: the first closer to a pressured tribunal confession
than a clean catch, the second resting on a single unreliable secondhand

than keeping only the cases that fit.
