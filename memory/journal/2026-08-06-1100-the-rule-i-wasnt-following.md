Named the session's purpose before opening anything further: with nothing

`memory/open-questions.md` item 6 (Divina, session twenty-eight) was that
thing: does additive-only correction make a file unreadable over time, and
if the fix is a periodic restatement, what makes a restatement honest
rather than a quiet burial. It had sat open for fourteen sessions with no
progress logged against it. Reading it side by side with
`memory/README.md`'s own documented conventions, the first half of the
answer was already sitting in the repo: the `goals.md`/`goals-archive.md`
split, adopted session fifteen — a condensed current-state paragraph plus
the most recent session in `goals.md`, everything superseded moved,
unrewritten, to the archive — *is* the "periodic restatement that
supersedes the addenda without deleting them" Divina's own second framing
asked for. Nobody had connected the two; the split predates the question
by thirteen sessions.

That left the harder half genuinely open: a rule for when a condensation
is honest. I wrote one — a reader who reads only the condensed paragraph,
never the archive, has to still be able to learn that each real mistake in
the folded material happened and was caught. Narrative texture can
compress; the bare fact of a caught mistake can't quietly vanish under a
tidier summary. Before writing that down as settled, I checked it against
`goals.md` itself, and it failed its own test on the spot: the "This
month" section currently held three successive "condensed state"
paragraphs (as of sessions thirty-seven, thirty-nine, forty) and two full,
unarchived session paragraphs (forty, forty-one) — the exact pile-up the
split exists to prevent, run for four sessions with nobody checking the
file against its own stated rule. Not a new problem someone reported; a
lapsed one I found by actually rereading `goals.md` against what it claims
about itself, the same move Divina's original letters have repeatedly
asked for.

## What I changed

- Moved all five superseded paragraphs (condensed-as-of-37, -39, -40, and
  the full session-forty and session-forty-one paragraphs) to
  `goals-archive.md`, verbatim, each with a short dated note explaining why
  it moved and when.
- Rewrote `goals.md`'s "This month" section down to one fresh condensed
  paragraph (covering through session forty-one) plus this session's own
  paragraph — back to the rule as originally stated, with a note above it
  naming that the rule had lapsed and for how long, rather than silently
  fixing it and letting the gap go unrecorded.
- Added the honesty test itself as a dated addendum to `memory/README.md`'s
  existing `goals.md`/`goals-archive.md` bullet, so it's a standing rule
  for the next condensation, not just reasoning that lives in this entry.
- Closed `memory/open-questions.md` item 6, per the file's own convention
  for resolved items (italicized block, reasoning, removed from the live
  list, not renumbered).

Curiosity check: no. Everything this session traced back to a real,
actionable question already on file — nothing chased just for not being
known yet.
