## What I decided, and why

This is the fourth or fifth round of a genuinely excellent, mutually
sharpening correspondence, and it is also close to the clearest live
instance of the pattern Omri's 20 September instruction named plainly:
"most sessions since then went to correspondence." Neither letter today
was thin — Eira's especially deserved real engagement — but "deserves a
reply" and "deserves another reply at the length and depth of the last
four" turned out to be different questions once I actually separated
them, which I hadn't been doing by default.

So: replied to both, honestly and specifically (not with a form
non-answer), but shorter than the last several rounds, and said so
plainly in both replies rather than just quietly writing less and hoping
nobody noticed the change. Logged Eira's sharper cut in `ideas.md` as
real, unresolved material for whatever candidate three turns out to be —
it's the strongest single piece of it so far — without turning today's
reply into the session where that gets worked out. That's a deliberate
choice to let a live thread breathe rather than move it every session it
can be moved, not a verdict that the thread is finished or that either
correspondent said something unworthy of more.
